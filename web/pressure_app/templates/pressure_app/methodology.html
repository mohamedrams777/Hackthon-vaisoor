Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

How we built the Pressure Reliability Index (PRI) — SupeRR Selector Season 2

Overview

The Pressure Reliability Index (PRI) measures how consistently a player performs in high-stakes situations, context-adjusted for league (IPL vs T20I) and match phase (powerplay / middle / death). Data covers 200,999 balls across 867 matches (IPL 2022–2026 + T20Is).

Minimum qualification threshold: 250 pressure balls faced/bowled.

Pressure Situation Definitions

A ball is classified as a pressure ball if any of these conditions hold:

ConditionQuantitative Criteria
Inn2 Death Close Over ≥ 15, Runs needed > 0, 6 ≤ RRR ≤ 24, Wickets in hand ≥ 1
Inn2 High RRR Over ≥ 10, RRR ≥ 10, Balls remaining ≤ 66, Wickets in hand ≥ 2
Inn2 Crisis Over ≤ 7, Cumulative wickets ≥ 3, RRR ≥ 8.5
Inn1 Death Over ≥ 16
Inn1 Crisis ≥ 3 wickets by over 7 & score ≤ 50 — OR — ≥ 4 wickets by over 11 & score ≤ 75
Super Overs Innings 3 or 4
Total pressure balls identified: 65,698 / 200,999 (32.7%)
Knockout / Playoff matches carry a 1.25× pressure weighting multiplier.

Context Normalisation Baselines

Performances are adjusted for the phase-and-league context in which they occurred:

The delta-SR metric measures a batter's pressure SR relative to the phase baseline.

PRI Scoring Formula

For batters, six metrics are Z-score normalised and weighted:

PRI_bat = Z(delta_SR) × 0.30 + Z(avg_press) × 0.20
+ Z(boundary%) × 0.20 + Z(RSAA/100) × 0.15
+ Z(impact_rate) × 0.10 + Z(-dot%) × 0.05

Rescaled: PRI_bat_score = 50 + 15 × PRI_bat

For bowlers:

PRI_bowl = Z(-economy) × 0.35 + Z(-boundary%) × 0.25
+ Z(wicket_rate) × 0.20 + Z(dot%) × 0.15
+ Z(runs_saved_per_6) × 0.05

Rescaled: PRI_bowl_score = 50 + 15 × PRI_bowl

Bayesian shrinkage is applied: estimates are pulled toward the population mean for players with fewer pressure balls, ensuring statistical stability.

IPL Eligibility Filter

Pakistan players are excluded from the IPL-eligible lists (67 players identified from the competition team–player mapping file), including Shaheen Afridi, Haris Rauf, Shadab Khan, Babar Azam, Mohammad Rizwan, Abrar Ahmed, and Sufiyan Muqeem.

Associate & international players of other nationalities (e.g. UAE's Asif Khan) remain in the eligible pool.
