How the Pitch Plays:

Pace and carry early, then slows and grips in overs 7–15. Least swing of any IPL ground in 2026. Bounce truer under lights; dew helps the chase. Square boundaries longer than straight ones.

BEFORE THE RETENTION DEADLINE: Four Numbers to Check Against the Plan
  1. Burger out, Sandeep kept. Burger's death-over economy was 9.9 against Sandeep's 12.7. Sushant Mishra (22 SMAT wickets) becomes the only left-arm seamer.
  2. Bishnoi out. He took 36 wickets in 36 games over 24 months, more than any spinner kept, leaving Jadeja, Punja and Puthur to carry the spin.
  3. Buybacks are a gamble. With no Right to Match, Hetmyer's T20 World Cup form could push his price back up.
  4. Overseas count. Five kept with Curran. Only one of the two XI gaps can be filled from overseas, and a Hetmyer buyback would make seven of eight.
