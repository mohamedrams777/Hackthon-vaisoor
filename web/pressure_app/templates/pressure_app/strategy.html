Jaipur Blueprint: How to Win
1
Win the Toss, Bowl First

Chasers won 75% of games in 2026. 2nd innings powerplay runs 1.4 runs/over faster due to dew.

2
Spin the Middle (Overs 7–15)

Deploy Jadeja and Punja for 8 overs. Avoid right-arm pace in middle overs where it leaks 10.8 rpo.

3
Pace at the Ends Only

Archer with the hard new ball; Curran's cutters & Gap-2 specialist at the death.

Chase 220+ with Deep Batting
